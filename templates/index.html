Deep Learning Architecture

5-Stage CNN Backbone

Extracts fine spatial pulmonary features, opacities, and edges progressively across channels 32 to 512, downsampling high-resolution radiographs into a dense 7x7 spatial feature grid.

Vision Transformer Encoder

Applies 8-head self-attention with learnable positional embeddings across 49 visual tokens. Captures long-range bilateral lung relationships that traditional CNNs often miss.

Class-Weighted Loss & AdamW

Trained with inverse frequency class-weighting and AdamW optimizer with cosine learning rate scheduling to address medical dataset imbalances across COVID, Normal, and Viral Pneumonia.
